# Product Vision

Left Traffic is where aircraft live.

Not pilots.

Not owners.

Aircraft.

Every aircraft has a story.

Every owner contributes to that story.

When ownership changes, the story continues.

The aircraft is the permanent entity.

Ownership is simply one relationship in the aircraft's lifetime.

The emotional center of the application is pride of ownership.

Users should feel the same satisfaction opening Left Traffic that they feel entering the pattern on a Saturday morning.

Every design decision should reinforce this feeling.

---

## V1 framing

This remains the long-term direction. It is not being reversed.

V1, as scoped in `docs/MVP_SCOPE.md`, is deliberately simpler: a feed for
posting and sharing aviation photos and videos, with likes and comments.
Aircraft profiles exist, but as something you create and tag, not the app's
organizing principle. Posting doesn't require owning an aircraft.

This is sequencing, not a change of thesis. A network needs people and
content before "the aircraft is the permanent entity, ownership is one
chapter" means anything to a user — that idea needs other owners, other
chapters, other posts to point at. V1 bootstraps the people and the content.
The aircraft-centric identity — Aircraft Detail as a story, ownership as a
chapter, pride of ownership as the emotional center — is the intended v2+
layer once there's a user base for it to matter to.

Don't read the presence of this section as license to keep drifting v1
further from the vision above. If a future milestone can't articulate how it
leads back toward this thesis, that's worth a product conversation before
building it.
